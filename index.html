About Us

BUS Game Devs (BUSGDA) is a not-for-profit organization that brings the local Vancouver game development community together with a variety of events each year. Originally founded by students from UBC, SFU, and BCIT, BUS has run the two largest community game jams in the province since 2019.

Our events are opportunities to gather over a shared passion for game development and a desire to grow, learn and improve together! We throw Global Game Jam Vancouver, The Vancouver Game Dev Series, Game Book Clubs and all sorts of Game Dev focused events!

Since its start in April 2023, Vancouver Game Dev Series (VGDS) has brought fantastic speakers,industry veterans and hobbyists to learn and share from their experience. It has expanded to host guests from other parts of Canada and the US.
