About Us

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you're interested in volunteering, sponsoring our programs, partnering on an event, or just learning more about what we do, don't hesitate to reach out. Our community is built by people who are passionate about games, creativity, and helping others grow, and we're always excited to connect with new collaborators. Send us a message and let's talk about how we can work together to support and strengthen the game development community.
